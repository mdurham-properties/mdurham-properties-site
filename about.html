About M.Durham

Built on trust, shaped by experience, and dedicated to helping people feel at home.

Our Story

M.Durham Properties & Rentals exists to make home decisions simpler and kinder. Our founder, Milo Durham, grew up watching his family struggle with housing—an experience that became the spark for a different kind of real estate service: one that is patient, transparent, and focused on people first.

Today, we help renters and buyers navigate the market with clarity—offering honest guidance, responsive communication, and support from the first viewing to the final signature.

Meet the Founder

Milo Durham leads with empathy and a hands‑on approach. He believes that everyone deserves a path to a safe, comfortable home—and that great service starts with listening. When you work with us, you’ll feel informed, respected, and understood.
